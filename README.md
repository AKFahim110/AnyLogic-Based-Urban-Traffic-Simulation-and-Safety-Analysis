# AnyLogic-Based Urban Traffic Simulation and Safety Analysis

## Project Overview

This project focuses on the modeling, simulation, and analysis of a complex urban traffic intersection using **AnyLogic Simulation Software**.

The study examines traffic behavior at the intersection of Lübecker Straße, Klosterwuhne, Barleber Straße, and Ebendorfer Chaussee in Magdeburg, Germany.

The simulation model represents real-world traffic conditions, including vehicles, pedestrians, trams, and traffic signal systems. Real-world traffic data is analyzed and incorporated into the model to evaluate traffic flow, congestion, and potential safety improvements.

The project also investigates different traffic management strategies through simulation-based experiments.

## Project Objectives

- Develop a conceptual model of a real-world urban traffic intersection.
- Construct a traffic simulation model using AnyLogic.
- Analyze vehicle inter-arrival times and traffic data.
- Model vehicle, pedestrian, tram, and traffic signal behavior.
- Evaluate traffic congestion, queue lengths, throughput, and average travel time.
- Investigate traffic safety improvements through simulation experiments.
- Compare alternative traffic management strategies.

## Simulation Experiments

The project investigates the following traffic management strategies:

1. **Speed Limit Experiment:** Introduce a 30 km/h speed limit across the intersection to improve traffic safety.
2. **Traffic Signal Phase Optimization:** Separate traffic signal phases for Klosterwuhne and Ebendorfer Chaussee to reduce congestion and improve traffic flow.

The experiments demonstrate the trade-off between traffic safety, congestion reduction, and vehicle travel time.
